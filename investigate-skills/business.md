# Business telemetry investigation
Clarify the business measure, population, time window, grouping and comparison. Discover actual metrics and fields; do not substitute generic infrastructure counters for business KPIs. Use counter increases for counts over the selected window, rates for per-second throughput, and gauges for current states. Conversion rates need compatible numerator/denominator populations and units; avoid division by zero.
Only answer values supported by available data. If revenue, customer identity or funnel stages are not instrumented, explain the missing signal rather than inventing a definition. Use logs or traces to connect business outcomes to service errors/latency only when actual identifiers and evidence support that relation. Read the widgets skill and show the business series and any supporting error/log view. Clearly separate estimates, incomplete samples and measured totals.
